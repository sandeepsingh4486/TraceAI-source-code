# 60-Second Pitch

Engineering change starts as messy language, but delivery requires structured decisions.

A customer sends a PDF, an ECN, an email, or meeting notes. Before a team can act, an experienced engineer or project manager has to extract requirements, identify affected components, create risks, draft tests, and build traceability. That can take hours.

**TraceAI turns that first pass into minutes.**

You upload the engineering change. AI converts it into atomic requirements, acceptance criteria, cross-functional impacts, risks, test cases, a traceability matrix, actions, and clarification questions.

The important part is what the system *doesn't* do: it does not invent missing requirements or claim engineering approval. It marks inference, exposes ambiguity, and keeps a human in the loop.

TraceAI is not another chatbot. It is a structured semantic transformation pipeline from **engineering change to review-ready traceability**.
