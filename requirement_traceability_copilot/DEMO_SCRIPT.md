# 3-Minute Demo Script

## 0:00–0:20 — Problem

"Engineering changes rarely arrive in a clean database. They arrive as PDFs, emails, ECNs, meeting notes, and specifications. A project manager or engineering lead can spend hours converting those documents into requirements, impacts, risks, tests, and action items."

## 0:20–0:40 — Product

"This is TraceAI. I give it the raw engineering change. AI performs semantic analysis and returns a structured, traceable engineering draft. It is not a chatbot and it does not approve the design."

## 0:40–1:20 — Input → requirements

Open the bundled ECN and click Analyze.

"Here the customer asks for a configurable load pre-warning, overload logging, CAN reporting, and fail-safe behavior. TraceAI breaks that into atomic requirements and attaches evidence, acceptance criteria, and ambiguity."

Point to one ambiguity.

"Notice it does not invent the missing detail. It asks us to clarify it."

## 1:20–1:55 — Impact + risks

Open Impact.

"One line in a customer document can affect firmware, HMI, CAN, non-volatile memory, and verification. TraceAI makes those relationships visible and marks inferred impacts separately from explicit requirements."

Open Risks.

"Now the project team gets an immediate RAID starting point — for example, undefined overload-event semantics could fill storage with duplicates."

## 1:55–2:25 — Tests + traceability

Open a fault-injection test, then Traceability.

"Each drafted test links back to one or more requirement IDs. This is the real value: the output is structured and traceable, not just a summary."

## 2:25–2:45 — Hours to minutes

Point to the metric.

"Our baseline for this demo is three hours of manual first-pass analysis. The AI creates a first draft in minutes or seconds, while final engineering review remains with qualified people."

## 2:45–3:00 — Close

"TraceAI turns engineering change into review-ready traceability. The next step is integration with Jira/ALM tools and organization-specific requirement templates."
